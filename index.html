THE GAME

Start as a lone Warrior in the cosy town of Askvakt Outpost, then follow the dirt road north through five zones, from the sunny forest to the fallen kingdom. Hunt monsters, topple bosses, gather materials and build your hero. Everything you see and hear (art, music and sound) is generated by the game itself.

⚔

REAL-TIME COMBAT

Tab-target combat with Rage, powerful abilities, a free Charge, shield blocks, bosses with telegraphed attacks and an action bar you can arrange yourself.

🌲

FIVE ZONES

The Forest, the Shadowwood, the Iron Peaks, the Ashen Wastes and the Fallen Kingdom. Walk straight from one to the next, and unlock waypoints to teleport back.

👑

LOOT & ENCHANTING

Common to Mythic gear, item comparison tooltips, repairs, and an Enchanter who makes your best items a little better with materials you mine and chop.

⛏

PROFESSIONS

Learn Mining and Woodcutting, gather materials in every zone and sell or use them for enchanting.

🏠

YOUR OWN HOME

Buy one of the old village houses, decorate nothing but enjoy a warm fireplace and a 100-slot personal chest for everything you want to keep.

🏆

ACHIEVEMENTS & TITLES

Over 55 achievements, stat points to spend on every level, mounts to ride and titles to show next to your name.

HOW TO PLAY

Download the game with the big button above.
Unzip the file anywhere (right-click → Extract All).
Open the folder and run DarkRealm.exe. If Windows shows a warning, choose More info → Run anyway.
Create a character and walk north out of town. Have fun!
